# Revised strategy: evidence, relevance and measurement

## Diagnosis

Phase 1 removed important discovery friction and created a readable starting point. Further robots directives are unlikely to be the highest-value next change. The remaining work is to make the service offer more specific, strengthen the provenance of the cases, resolve the founder identity, and obtain legitimate confirmation outside the studio's own site.

The completed50-query exploratory sample contains zero Joseph mentions. It spans two periods with a new Google profile between them, so it is not a clean baseline. A future complete run under consistent conditions establishes a post-Phase1/profile starting point. No causal claim about Phase1 is possible. See17-benchmark-report.md.

## P0: establish the starting point

- Freeze the current deployment while collecting the first 50 answers. Record run conditions and raw responses before coding or submitting new indexing requests.
- Retain www canonical origin, existing sitemap, robots rules and server-readable content. Recheck the public URLs and preserve evidence of the checks.
- Resolve the person entity: the owner now supplies Yusuf Yakubov; public copy currently says Joseph. Introduce Yusuf Yakubov, known as Joseph, on the founder page and in Person data after approval of the wording.
- Keep CAD 700–1,000/month as a scoped service range, with ad spend separate. Do not imply a full website, unlimited video or every service at the floor price.
- Separate screenshot-supported metrics, owner-reported lead counts, and unavailable revenue/booking results.
- Treat beauty as a service offered; there is no verified beauty client case in the inspected portfolio.

## P1: answer the actual buying questions

Create four differentiated service pages (Meta Ads, Reels, creative advertising, websites) and one shared service-area page. Extend existing pricing, industry and founder pages instead of duplicating them at new URLs. Add a lightweight case index so all four case pages are discoverable even without expanding a portfolio card.

For each service: a clear summary; suitable customer; problem; candidate deliverables and their agreed limits; current pricing; separate media spend; true service area; relevant evidence with limits; direct contact; simple CTA. The first paragraph should help an owner decide whether to keep reading, not merely contain search phrases.

Use genuine case imagery, the existing typography and colours, and concise text. Add relevant image thumbnails to evidence blocks so the new pages are recognisably part of this portfolio. Keep the existing homepage artwork and jar/music behaviour.

## P2: independent confirmation and learning in public

The strongest next corroboration is a client's public acknowledgement of the actual work, supported by a real signed-off case or review. An owner-created social profile improves identity consistency but is not independent validation. A directory listing is discovery infrastructure; it is not equivalent to editorial coverage or a verified review.

Run an honest content series about the experiment. It can demonstrate the process over time, but cannot establish expertise, results or recommendations that have not occurred.

## P3

No extra AI files, hidden messages, synthetic reviews, keyword city grids or claimed ranking schema. Revisit optional experiments only after indexation, useful content and measurement are working.

## Decision rules

No crawl/index eligibility → fix access.
Eligible but not indexed → inspect discovery, canonical selection and content usefulness in Search Console.
Indexed but rarely mentioned → examine query fit and corroboration from actually cited competitors.
Mentions but few visits → inspect how the answer describes the studio and links to it.
Visits without relevant enquiries → inspect offer, evidence, contact usability and audience.
A change in counts is an observation, not proof the website edit caused it.
