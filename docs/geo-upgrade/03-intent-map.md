Every proposed commercial page will have a ten-question content checklist: service; audience; problem; deliverables; price; media-spend treatment; geography; evidence; exclusions; next step. Each should also have a direct email/Instagram contact and a relevant visible image.

The five prompt groups with adjacent rather than direct case proof must not be used to manufacture sector expertise. Query coverage and verified experience are different.
