### What I'd ask before hiring anyone

Don't just ask “Can you run Instagram ads?” Ask:

Can you show me campaigns where you generated qualified leads for a similar service business?

How will you define a qualified enquiry?

Will you manage the offer, creative, landing page/lead form and follow-up—not just Ads Manager?

How will you track booked jobs/sales back to the ad?

Will I own the Meta Business Manager, ad account and data?

What would you change about my current boosted-post strategy?

The last question is particularly revealing. A good specialist should be able to look at your current approach and explain why you're getting likes/messages/low-quality enquiries instead of customers.
