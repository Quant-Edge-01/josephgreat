# Google and Apple discovery profiles — recommendation, not a registration

Researched 13 September 2026. No profile created, edited or submitted; no ad spend authorized by this recommendation.

## Google Business Profile first, if eligible

Google Ads is paid advertising. Google Business Profile is the relevant local profile for this organic-discovery task. A business that actually visits customers can qualify as a service-area business. If Joseph films at client premises and does not receive clients at his own address, use one service-area profile with the address hidden from customers. Confirm actual in-person service before submitting. An online-only business is not eligible merely because it targets Toronto.

Sources: [Google eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en), [service-area rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en).

## Apple Business second

Apple's current support page accepts businesses operating virtually as well as those with physical locations. Account/brand eligibility should not be represented as proof that a particular address-less studio qualifies for a Maps place card. Verify the relevant Canadian listing workflow before submission; never add a fictitious storefront or expose a home address to manufacture eligibility.

Sources: [Apple Business FAQ](https://business.apple.com/support), [location setup](https://support.apple.com/guide/business/add-a-single-location-abcb98816a34/1/web/1).
