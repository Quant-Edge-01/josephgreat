## Consistency and editorial rules

Use the entity specification: brand, full founder name where relevant, canonical www website, Toronto/GTA area, factual services and public business email. Respect platform-specific fields and description restrictions.

Ask a real customer for an honest account, never a prescribed rating or review from someone without experience. Do not trade incentives for a positive review. No mass directory submissions, fake biographies or bot-generated testimonials.

Client or partner text should identify the actual project role; it should not claim outcomes that the client cannot verify. Do not copy the studio's description into a supposedly independent article and present it as reporting.

## Wikipedia

Do not pursue an article now. The inspected evidence does not establish substantial independent coverage. Own website, social audience and analytics screenshots do not satisfy that requirement. Revisit only if independent editorial sources actually emerge.

Record candidate URL, owner/contact, exact claim to corroborate, independence class, cost/terms, permission status, public URL, date verified and whether the source was ever cited in benchmark answers. A mention is not automatically a useful citation.
