# Case-study evidence checklist

## Evidence tiers

S = visible in saved screenshot; O = owner/project reported; I = interpretation; M = missing.
Evidence stored by the business is primary evidence. Hosting an analytics screenshot does not create independent corroboration.

## Proposed case structure

1. Category, area and business problem.
2. What Joseph says he delivered.
3. Saved aggregate ad metrics with source caption.
4. Organic creative evidence separately.
5. Reporting period and missing inputs.
6. Limits: conversations are not unique qualified customers, appointments or revenue.
7. Supported lesson: separately measuring replies, qualification and bookings is needed to evaluate the next test. This is an interpretation, not a historical result.
8. Direct link to the relevant service and contact.

Replace blanket language that all figures came from screenshots when the nine-lead claim is owner-reported.

## Other evidence

- Spartan Gymnastics: 9,224 views, 74.9% non-followers, two-week/$0-ad-spend case; no measured enrolment or membership growth.
- YouTube: saved 33,134,030 lifetime views; +324.4K metric is historical net subscriber growth, not a current live total. Award is visible in saved photo, not evidence of agency client outcomes.
- Quantlarper: owned project, 1.2M 30-day views, 175 active email subscribers. Subscribers must never become paid clients in copy.
- Beauty/restaurant/retail: adjacent capability evidence only. No fictional direct case, testimonial, client logo or outcomes.

## Minimum evidence for future updates

Keep date, source, redacted export/screenshot, exact metric definition, scope, responsible person, written publication permission where needed, and whether a claim is independently corroborated. Preserve the original snapshot; do not replace it with a more impressive number lacking provenance.
