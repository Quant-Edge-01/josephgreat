# Source-first analysis — saved ChatGPT benchmark

The raw 50-attempt benchmark contains literal URLs in 48 captured responses. Mechanical extraction found 123 unique domains and 197 URL occurrences:

| Source class | URL occurrences | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Company, client or local owned sites | 190 | Provider websites overwhelmingly supplied visible evidence |
| Directory/review platforms | 5 | Clutch and a few directory/review URLs appeared directly |
| ChatGPT/internal | 2 | Product/internal links, not corroboration |
| Community/social with literal URL | 0 | UI labels/map captures may omit URLs; this is not proof of absence |

Frequent literal domains included `mybloomhq.com` (7 occurrences/six prompts), `metabaz.ca` (7/six), `bowocreative.com` (7/one), `orcafy.com` (5/five), then several provider domains at four occurrences. `clutch.co` appeared three times across two prompts.

These are URL occurrences, not citation quality or causal selection evidence. One answer can repeat a link; saved Maps/source-label capture is incomplete.

## Strategy

Maintain the now-indexed, quotable owned pages and earn a few strong third-party records tied to real client experience. P0 is a client-controlled acknowledgement or honest Google review from Dream, plus any verifiable downstream outcome. P1 is a truthful Clutch/Sortlist-style record only when a real client can support verification. Do not manufacture Reddit threads, mass-submit thin directories or count syndication of one review as many independent reviews.
