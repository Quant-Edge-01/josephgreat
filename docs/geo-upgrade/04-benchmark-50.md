# 50-prompt benchmark — v1.0

Created 12 September 2026. Five non-branded prompts per category. These are test inputs, not observed answers. Baseline is after deployed Phase 1 (7d84944), before additional work. No mention rate exists yet.

## A. General Toronto agency discovery

Query context: Toronto. This does not establish a physical search location.

- **A1:** I run a small business in Toronto and need an affordable marketing agency. Who should I look at?
- **A2:** Can you suggest a small Toronto marketing studio where I would work directly with the person creating the content?
- **A3:** I'm opening a local business in Toronto and need help explaining what we do online. Which agencies should I compare?
- **A4:** I want a smaller creative agency in Toronto, not a huge corporate firm. What are some options?
- **A5:** Who in Toronto can help a small business with social content, ads and its website together?

## B. Budget-constrained discovery
