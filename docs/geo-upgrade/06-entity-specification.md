## Application

Homepage: concise founder/service wording within an existing text block.
About: full name and alias, owned projects, named source limitations.
Footer: short brand/geography line, not the whole paragraph.
Organization: compact description, founder reference, areaServed and public email contactPoint.
Person: full name, alternateName, stable ID; owned profiles only.
Service: service-specific name/type/description; same organization provider.
Profiles: adapt the description to the platform rather than pasting full paragraphs everywhere.

Google Business Profile descriptions have separate rules: do not paste the price line or website URL into the description. Keep website/contact in designated fields. Do not add an address or opening hours that are not established.
